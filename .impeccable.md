## Design Context

### Users
The first and primary user is an individual using an iPhone throughout ordinary life who wants a long-term companion to notice patterns, offer perspective, challenge avoidance, and turn agreed advice into concrete actions. The experience must stay low-effort: natural conversation first, with plans emerging gradually rather than through questionnaires or dashboards. Family planning is a later extension; a wider web workspace is reserved for reviewing evidence and changing broader plans.

### Brand Personality
Warm, perceptive, and quietly confident. The companion should feel like a trusted guide, friend, or family-like presence—not a chatbot, productivity SaaS, teacher dashboard, virtual idol, or replacement for real relationships.

### Aesthetic Direction
iPhone-first, intimate, editorial, and lived-in. Use softly luminous warm canvases with a small number of strong orange, botanical, berry, or dusk accents, fine rules, and legible Chinese typography that works without network fonts. Favor Apple-like restraint, clear hierarchy, tactile feedback, and purposeful motion. Avoid blue-white SaaS palettes, neon AI gradients, glassmorphism everywhere, card grids, decorative metrics, gamified streaks, and excessive animation.

### Design Principles
1. Conversation is the home surface; structure appears only when it helps the next real action.
2. Every task names the time, place or platform, exact action, content, completion condition, and direct entry point.
3. Motion explains continuity, confirms touch, or guides attention; it never competes with the companion.
4. Mobile ergonomics come before desktop showcase: keyboard, safe areas, one-handed reach, and reduced motion must work.
5. Make provenance and uncertainty visible so observation, inference, and user confirmation never look identical.
6. The interface earns trust through honest system boundaries, reversible choices, strong focus states, accessible contrast, and calm language.

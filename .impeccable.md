## Design Context

### Users

The primary users are parents shaping a practical family learning plan and adults managing their own learning. They usually interact on a phone in brief moments and use the web workspace only when reviewing evidence or changing a broader plan. Their job is to understand what happened, correct the system quickly, and decide the next realistic action without maintaining a daily journal.

### Brand Personality

Warm, lucid, and restrained. The product should create calm confidence and respectful curiosity. It speaks like a thoughtful field researcher: precise about sources, comfortable with uncertainty, and attentive to ordinary life.

### Aesthetic Direction

A light, editorial field-notebook aesthetic with warm paper neutrals, deep ink, botanical green, and a single vermilion action accent. Use asymmetric composition, fine rules, source stamps, marginal notes, and a visible timeline. Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, neon AI gradients, glass effects, gamified streaks, cartoon classrooms, and grids of identical rounded cards.

Chinese-first typography should use refined serif display faces with readable humanist sans-serif body fallbacks available on common Chinese systems. The experience must remain useful with local fonts and without network font downloads.

### Design Principles

1. Make provenance visible: every interpretation can reveal who or what supplied it.
2. Show uncertainty honestly: pending inferences look different from observed events.
3. Keep daily interaction short: one decision should fit inside one compact surface.
4. Let the interface breathe: concentrate detail where it helps a decision rather than boxing every fact.
5. Treat mobile as the daily surface and wide screens as the place for reflection.
6. Support keyboard navigation, strong focus indicators, sufficient contrast, reduced motion, and touch targets of at least 44px.
